ZentaCircuits does not require an account and does not ask for personal details to read any page.

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It collects anonymous usage data such as pages viewed and device type.

**Advertising.** We may show ads from Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we store your email only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

**Simulators** run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to our servers.

Questions: [contact@zentacircuits.com](mailto:contact@zentacircuits.com). This policy will be reviewed before launch.
