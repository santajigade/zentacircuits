ZentaCircuits teaches electronics the way engineers learn it on the bench: understand the theory, run the circuit, then test yourself.

Every topic page has a short answer, clear diagrams, the key formulas, a worked example in real units and, where it fits, a live simulator that runs in your browser. Simulators are checked against textbook equations before they go live.

The site is written by [Santaji Gadepatil](/author/santaji-gadepatil/), an electronics and telecommunication engineer. It is part of the Zenta family of free engineering resources.

Everything here is free to read, with no sign-up.
