**Facts from real sources.** Every lesson cites at least three references: standard textbooks, manufacturer datasheets and application notes, or university lectures.

**Checked numbers.** Worked examples are calculated by script, so the numbers in the text match the maths.

**Tested simulators.** Each simulator ships with test cases taken from textbook equations. A simulator is published only when every test passes.

**Named authors.** Every page names its author. We update pages when a fact changes and show the updated date.

**Corrections.** Found a mistake? Write to us from the [contact page](/contact/) and we will fix it.
